The name

Hit + Pet = HitterPetter

Say it out loud and you get Hitar Petar (Хитър Петър), "Clever Peter," the sly trickster of Bulgarian storytelling.

In English it reads as our promise: we'll provoke you, then we'll warm you up.

A team of free-spirited artists with no limits on creativity, who never take themselves too seriously.

About

HitterPetter Productions is a Los Angeles–based creative house for people who like to mix things up: film and festivals, Hollywood and the Balkans, high art and a good party.

We produce award-winning short films, concerts and festivals, and we've taken a fur-covered art car to the open desert. The goal is to give artists a bigger stage, to build a bridge between the U.S. and Bulgaria, and to make work that surprises people.

L.A. is a melting pot, and we wanted to bring something to it that nobody else could. So we draw on our Bulgarian roots, folklore included, as a source of originality. Then we mix them with film, electronic music, festival culture and whatever else sparks an idea.

It started in 2012 with a few friends who wanted to make things together. A weekly dance night grew into Balkan Fest, a secret festival in a hidden San Diego canyon. That led to a mobile stage with twelve-foot horns, and to films that went on to win at festivals from Beverly Hills to Dhaka.

A Bulgarian actress chasing her Hollywood break brings her nine-year-old son to an audition, and he books the part instead. Art car · Mobile stage

The Bulgarian Kuker Art Car

Metal & fur & fire.

A moving sculpture and a festival on wheels, with an orchestrated light show and flame effects timed to the music. A collective of Bulgarian and American artists built it, and HitterPetter Productions produces and manages it.

5,000 yrsKukeri tradition, reloaded
12 ftFire-breathing horns
15,000 WSound, pro DJ booth & live-band stage
35–40Riders on board

The Kuker borrows its look from Bulgaria's kukeri, masked dancers who have been scaring off evil spirits with horns, fur and bells for some 5,000 years. We kept the horns and the fur, then added a 15,000-watt sound system, a pro DJ booth, a live-band stage, LED light and flame effects.

In 2016 the Balkan Fest organizing team went to Burning Man together. On the way home, Petar Ivanov "Petsata" and Rado Raychev said the crew would bring its own art car the next year. Petro and Maria answered at once: "It'll be a Kuker!"

The Kuker (a.k.a. The Disco Daemon) is "a mobile stage for spreading Bulgarian DNA through the 21st century." It runs an orchestrated show of sound, light, fire and atmospherics, and works as a roaming party, a stationary dance spot, or a late-night stage once the main stages shut down.

Festivals · Music · Happenings

Gatherings where the audience joins in

Since 2012 the team has created events where the line between performer and audience disappears: secret festivals, sold-out concerts and one-night happenings. We started on our own, worked through the nonprofit PCHELA, and have run as HitterPetter Productions since 2016.

Co-founded 2013 · San Diego · 2013, 2014, 2016–2019

Balkan Fest – Europa Roots

In 2012 we visited a flamenco festival that our friend Basilio Ceravolo has hosted for about two decades, at his estate in a hidden canyon in the heart of San Diego. World-class performers and the audience mix like one big family at a village fair. Petro Dushkov, Maria Bobeva, Basilio Ceravolo and Maria Breshkova decided to bring that model to the Balkans.

The idea was a mini Koprivshtitsa: a small Bulgarian island where young and old, artists and fans live together for a long weekend in late May. Maria Bobeva was artistic director and turned the canyon into a Bulgarian village. Basilio and Maria Breshkova ran logistics and volunteers. Everyone attending volunteered too, in the kitchen, at registration and on clean-up. Guests took dance classes with leading Bulgarian, Greek, Romanian and Turkish choreographers, plus workshops in Troyan pottery, embroidery, kuker-mask making, and weaving on a custom-built Chiprovtsi loom. The location stayed secret and was revealed bit by bit in a private group, so only the truly curious made it down the rabbit hole.

Tagline: Different Tribes, One Spirit! Presented by Basilio, PCHELA and HitterPetter. One edition brought together three of Bulgaria's great gaida players, Vasil Bebelekov, Petar Yanev and Ivan Varimezov, with the voices of Valya Balkanska, Maria Bebelekova and Tsvetanka Varimezova.

Kuker Fest California

A gathering of kukeri "from all lands," organized by the Kuker Crew and led by Petro Dushkov. Elka and Dancho Ruskov of the Bulgarian Cultural and Heritage Center of Seattle supported it, along with Eli Yordanova, founder of the Festival of Bulgarian Arts in Toronto. The program featured Dimitar Minkov of the Dimitrovche ensemble (Toronto), Konstantin "Kotseto" Marinov of Chicago's Vereya festival, pianist and composer Milen Kirov, Donka and Nikolay Kolev, Stoyan Kostov "Pileto" and Rumen Shopov.

Supported by the Bulgarian Cultural and Heritage Center of Seattle, the Herb Alpert Music Center (LACC), Bulgarian Traditions NPC-Chicago, ElArte-Toronto, the Consulate General of Bulgaria in L.A. and the National Culture Fund of Bulgaria. Powered by the Kuker art car, the day included a Kuker lab and theatre for kids and adults, a kukeri seminar, mask and embroidery workshops, a campfire jam and an evening gala concert with DEXTЪR, the Varimezovs and the Horo-San Band.

The headliner was electronic producer Ivan Shopov with the vocal duo Avigeya. Lora Shopova's visuals were projection-mapped onto fabric sails built by Filip Petkov (DJ PHEEL). It all played out in a 300-seat amphitheater that Basilio built by hand from recycled concrete blocks.

Longtime collaborators · Los Angeles

Milen Kirov & Orkestar Mézé

Pianist, composer and educator Milen Kirov is one of HitterPetter's closest creative partners. Born in Plovdiv into a musical family, he holds a doctorate from CalArts and a master's in composition from CSU Northridge, and teaches piano, composition and theory at Los Angeles City College. His music sits where Western classical, American jazz and Bulgarian traditional music meet. He has played and recorded with Perry Farrell, Money Mark and James Gadson, and released the solo piano album Spatium in 2022.

In 2011 he founded Orkestar Mézé, an 11-piece "peasant funk" band with a six-piece horn section and members from six countries. It mixes American funk and soul with Bulgarian odd meters. Their second album features Ellis Hall of Tower of Power and Theodosii Spassov.

With HitterPetter, Milen composed the original score for Audition and performed at Kuker Fest California, and on December 4, 2015 Orkestar Mézé shared the stage at Molly Malone's in Los Angeles with BALKANSKY (Theodosii Spassov, Ivan Shopov and Ivo Hristov), Ellis Hall and Miroslav Tadić.

Since January 2012 · Orange County

D.E.B.A. dance club

D.E.B.A. ("Dance Ethnos – Bulgarian Aerobics," or as we like to call it, Bulgarian Folk Aerobics) started half as a joke. It was a weekly folk-dance night Petro and Maria set up for friends, billed as a workout. It became the seed of everything that followed and connected the team with dancers, musicians and teachers across North America. Still going strong, with master classes from prominent guest artists, choreographers and educators: Petur Iliev, Gergana Panova, Elena Dimitrova, Daniela Ivanova-Nyberg, Prof. Anton Andonov, Prof. Nikolay Tsvetkov, Konstantin "Kotseto" Marinov, Tania Dimitrova, Iliana Bozhanova and Todor Yankov, Nina Kavardjikova, Diane Baker, Jaap Leegwater and many more.

The partners

HitterPetter is run by two creative partners who met through a shared love of art and culture. Since then they have built everything together: films, festivals, concerts and one very large horned art car.

Petro Dushkov

Founder & director · Producer

Petro is the founder and director of HitterPetter Productions, a Los Angeles–based production company that produces cultural projects in the U.S. and Bulgaria. The company gives Bulgarian artists a global stage and introduces international audiences to Bulgaria's rich cultural heritage and spirit.

He has lived in the U.S. for more than two decades. He co-founded the D.E.B.A. dance club (2012) and Balkan Fest (2013), and served as co-founder and director of the nonprofit PCHELA from 2013 to 2015. From December 2020 to October 2022 he served on the board of directors of BACES, the Bulgarian American Cultural Educational Society in Los Angeles, where he ran the cultural program and the annual calendar of events. He produced the award-winning shorts Audition, Blinks and Scarlet, and he produces the Kuker art car.

A self-taught gaida (bagpipe) player since his student days at the Technical University of Gabrovo, he has played everywhere from a landslide-wrecked villa on the Black Sea coast to the top of Yosemite Falls.

Maria Bobeva

Creative partner · Actress · Writer · Director

Maria is a Bulgarian actress known for her many roles at the State Satirical Theatre in Sofia. She graduated from the National Academy for Theatre and Film Arts, where she studied with the renowned professors Krikor Azaryan, Todor Kolev and Atanas Atanassov.

Her stage partners have included Stoyanka Mutafova, Georgi Kaloyanchev, Dimitar Manchev and Nikola Anastasov, as well as audience favorites Hristo Garbov, Plamen Sirakov, Filip Avramov and Krasi Radkov.

After a successful career with the Satirical Theatre, Maria moved to Hollywood to pursue film. There she has won numerous awards as an actress and as a screenwriter-director, with her films produced by her HitterPetter partner Petro Dushkov. She was also artistic director of Balkan Fest.
